# KBC Future video script

Target duration: 2 minutes. Spoken script below.

Your banking app tells you what you spent yesterday. But can it help you prepare for tomorrow?

KBC Future is a financial digital twin that anticipates cash-flow pressure and tests personalised responses before asking the customer to act.

It uses the customer’s transaction history to recognise income, bills and recurring transfers, estimate everyday spending, and forecast the next thirty days.

Meet Alex. Today, Alex has €1,940. That looks comfortable. But the forecast predicts a low point of €184—below Alex’s €250 safety buffer.

Using Alex’s own spending patterns, the engine proposes reducing dining by €70 and shopping by €10. Assuming these savings build gradually, the simulated minimum rises to €256.

But Alex might prefer cutting €40 from each category instead.

The LLM can explain the recommendation and propose that adjustment. The engine recalculates it before customer approval. The LLM cannot invent the financial result, and this MVP never moves money.

Different situations produce different responses. Sam has room for a €625 savings proposal. Robin’s irregular income calls for caution and review.

The learning calculation compares predicted and actual spending, then gradually adjusts future estimates.

Each forecast accepts only one customer’s data. Missing or stale history triggers review instead of false reassurance.

We’ve verified the approach with three synthetic customers and forty-three automated checks. Scaling across KBC is the next step.

KBC Future: understand the situation, anticipate the need, and help the customer choose what happens next.
